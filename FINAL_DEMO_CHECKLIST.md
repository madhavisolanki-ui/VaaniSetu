## 🎯 Pre-Demo Setup (1 Minute Before Judging)
1. **Device:** Android Tablet or Emulator (or Chrome browser displaying `http://127.0.0.1:8000`).
2. **Network State:** Turn ON Airplane Mode (or disable Wi-Fi/Ethernet) to demonstrate **100% genuine offline capability**.
3. **Volume:** Set device media volume to 80% so the judge can hear the authentic Santhali pronunciation.

## 🎬 Live Demonstration Sequence

### Step 1: Open the App & Demonstrate Offline-First Integrity (30 Seconds)
- Launch **VaaniSetu** on the tablet.
- Point to the **"● 100% Offline"** badge in the top-right app bar.
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"Respected Judges, VaaniSetu is built for 5,000+ primary schools in Jharkhand's tribal belt where internet is nonexistent and tablets have $\le$2GB RAM. Notice that our device is completely offline—zero cloud APIs, zero external servers."*

---

### Step 2: Live Teacher Speech Recording & ASR (45 Seconds)
- Ensure **Teacher • Hindi (शिक्षक • हिंदी)** is active.
- Tap the large central **Microphone** button.
- Speak clearly in Hindi: *"आज हम गिनती सीखेंगे।"* (or *"बच्चों, अपनी किताब खोलो।"*).
- Observe:
  1. Animated audio pulse rings around the mic.
  2. Elapsed recording timer (`00:01`, `00:02`, `00:03`).
- Tap the microphone button again to stop recording.
- Observe immediate transition:
  `Listening...` $\to$ `Processing speech...` $\to$ `Speech recognized` $\to$ `Translating...` $\to$ `Translation ready`.
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"The microphone captures 16kHz 16-bit Mono WAV audio. Our on-device audio engine calculates RMS voice energy to validate speech before running inference, ensuring classroom noise does not trigger false outputs."*

---

### Step 3: Multi-Script Santhali Translation & Audio Pronunciation (45 Seconds)
- Direct the judge's attention to the **Classroom Translation Card**:
  - **Recognized Speech:** *"आज हम गिनती सीखेंगे।"*
  - **Santhali Devanagari:** *"तेहेञ आबो लेखा चेदोक् आबोन।"*
  - **Santhali Ol Chiki:** *"ᱛᱮᱦᱮᱧ ᱟᱵᱚ ᱞᱮᱠᱷᱟ ᱪᱮᱫᱚᱜ ᱟᱵᱚᱱ᱾"*
  - **Phonetic Pronunciation:** *"Tehen abo lekha chedog abon."*
- Tap the **[ 🔊 Listen ]** button.
- Hear the authentic, clear Santhali classroom pronunciation clip play instantly.
- Tap **[ ⏹ Stop ]** to show instant responsive audio control.
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"Tribal children learn best when seeing their indigenous script alongside Devanagari. We provide dual-script rendering with phonetic guides and authentic audio clips recorded at 16kHz."*

---

### Step 4: 1-Tap Switching to Mundari and Ho (30 Seconds)
- Look at the **"जनजातीय भाषा"** selector bar on the screen:
  - Tap **"मुंडारी (ᱢᱩᱱᱰᱟᱨᱤ)"**:
    Card immediately updates to Mundari: *"तिसिंग आबु लेखा चेदुंग आबु।"*
  - Tap **"हो (ᱦᱳ)"**:
    Card immediately updates to Ho: *"तीसिंग अबु लेखा चेद अबु।"*
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"Jharkhand's classrooms have diverse tribal compositions across districts. A Hindi-medium teacher can toggle between Santhali, Mundari, and Ho instantly without restarting the app or re-entering data."*

---

### Step 5: Human Validation & Teacher Review Loop — Slide 8 Feature (45 Seconds)
- Tap the **"समीक्षा (Review)"** button on the translation card.
- A dialog opens: *"शिक्षक समीक्षा एवं सत्यापन (Teacher Validation & Dialect Correction)"*.
- Show the judge how a teacher can adjust the spelling to match a local block's dialect:
  - E.g., change word or add dialect note: *"खूंटी जिले की मुंडारी बोली के अनुसार"*.
- Tap **[ सत्यापित करें (Verify) ]**.
- The card immediately displays a green **"✓ शिक्षक द्वारा सत्यापित (Teacher Verified)"** badge.
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"AI can have dialect blindspots. As highlighted in Slide 8 of our presentation, VaaniSetu enforces human-in-the-loop validation, allowing teachers to review and approve translations for their specific village dialect."*

---

### Step 6: Bidirectional Dialogue — Student to Teacher (30 Seconds)
- Tap the **"Student • Tribal (छात्र • संथाली)"** toggle button.
- Tap the microphone button.
- Speak/simulate a student tribal phrase: *"आपानाः पुथी झिज पे।"*
- The card translates the phrase back to Hindi for the teacher: *"बच्चों, अपनी किताब खोलो।"*
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"Communication is a two-way street. VaaniSetu enables bidirectional classroom dialogue so teachers can understand what tribal students are saying in their mother tongue."*

---

### Step 7: NIPUN Bharat FLN Pedagogy Hub & Printable Worksheets (45 Seconds)
- Scroll down to the **"FLN शिक्षण सामग्री (Learning Content)"** card.
- Tap **"Worksheets (कार्यपत्रिका)"**:
  - Open Worksheet 1: *"गिनती सीखें (Learn Counting)"*.
  - Answer an interactive quiz question $\to$ show encouraging child feedback (*"बहुत अच्छा! 🎉"*).
  - Tap the **Print** icon in the app bar $\to$ show the **"निपुण भारत द्विभाषी कार्यपत्रिका"** printable view with student name, date, roll number, bilingual questions, and teacher signature section.
- Go back and tap **"Flashcards (फ़्लैशकार्ड)"**:
  - Swipe through bilingual flashcards (Numbers, Animals) with Ol Chiki script and 🔊 audio playback.
- Go back and tap **"Activities (गतिविधियाँ)"**:
  - Show child-friendly touch games with star reward scores (Score: 50).
- **Judge Pitch:**  
  *"VaaniSetu is not just a translation utility; it is a comprehensive NIPUN Bharat pedagogical suite providing bilingual worksheets, flashcards, and printable materials designed for early childhood FLN outcomes."*
